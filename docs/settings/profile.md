# Profile & Account

Manage your account information from the **Settings** page.

## Accessing Settings

Open your **account menu** at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**.

The page is organized into cards: **Profile**, **Notifications**, **Integrations**, **Website Settings**, and **Data & Privacy**.

## Editing Your Details

Profile fields are read-only until you click **Edit profile**. Make your changes, then click **Save changes** (or **Cancel** to discard).

## Profile

- **Name** — your first and last name, shown throughout Hyver
- **Email** — used for sign-in and notifications; this can't be changed
- **Phone** — optional, with a country selector. Use **Change phone** / **Update phone** to edit it.

Your avatar is generated automatically from your name or email.

## Default Chat Settings

New chats start with your **Default model**; you can change it per chat. See [Chat Settings](../chat/settings.md).

## Other Settings

The same Settings page also contains:

- **Notifications** — see [Notification Settings](../notifications/settings.md)
- **Integrations** — see [Integrations](../integrations/overview.md)
- **Website Settings** — theme and language; see [Theme & Language](appearance.md)
- **Data & Privacy** — download your data or delete your account; see [Data & Privacy](privacy.md)

## Deleting Your Account

Use **Delete account** under **Data & Privacy** — see [Data & Privacy](privacy.md). For subscription questions, open **Subscriptions** from the account menu or contact [support@hyver.app](mailto:support@hyver.app).

## Tips

- Keep your email current — it's where notifications go
