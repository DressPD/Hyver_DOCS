# Data & Privacy

Control your cookies and your data from **Settings → Data & Privacy**.

## Cookie Choices

The first time you visit Hyver, a **Your privacy choices** banner appears. You can **Accept all**, **Reject all**, or **Customise**, then **Save choices**.

| Category | What it covers |
|----------|----------------|
| **Strictly necessary** (always on) | Sign-in, security, remembering your cookie choice, and the theme and language you select |
| **Analytics** | Product analytics and performance monitoring that show which features are used and help diagnose errors. Never used for advertising. |

Change your mind any time with **Cookie preferences**. Once you're signed in, your choices are also saved to your account. Read our **Privacy Policy** for the full detail.

## Download Your Data

Click **Download** under **Download your data** to get a machine-readable copy (JSON) of everything Hyver holds about you, including your conversations. It may take a moment — the button shows **Preparing…** meanwhile.

## Delete Your Account

Click **Delete account** to permanently remove your account and personal data. **This cannot be undone.**

- It deletes your conversations, files, skills, tools, and integrations
- Billing records are kept for 7 years because the law requires it
- You confirm by typing the phrase shown, then click **Permanently delete**
- Deleted data can be restored from backups for up to 35 days, after which it's gone for good
- Some items need a manual step and are completed within one month; you'll get a reference number for them

You're signed out shortly after the deletion completes.
