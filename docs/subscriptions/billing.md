# Billing

> **Coming soon.** Paid plans aren't live yet. This describes how billing is set to work.

Hyver uses **Stripe** for payments and invoices.

## Managing Payment & Invoices

From your current-plan card on the **Subscriptions** page, click **Manage billing** to open the Stripe customer portal, where you can:

- Add, update, or remove payment methods
- Set a default card
- View and download past invoices

## Billing History

The Subscriptions page has a **Billing history** section. Click **Show** to list your recent invoices with their amount, status, date, and an **Invoice** link.

## Billing Cycle

- **Monthly plans** — charged on the same date each month
- **Annual plans** — charged once per year on your subscription anniversary (about 20% cheaper than paying monthly)

## Cancelling

Click **Cancel plan** on your current-plan card. Your access continues until the end of the current period, and the plan shows a "canceling at period end" note — you can **resume** any time before it ends.

## Refunds & Help

For billing questions or refund requests, contact [support@hyver.app](mailto:support@hyver.app) with your transaction details.
