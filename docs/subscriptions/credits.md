# Credits

> **Coming soon.** Paid plans and credit purchases aren't live yet. This describes how credits are set to work.

## What are Credits?

Credits are Hyver's usage currency, counted in **actions**:

- Each AI operation consumes actions
- Your **weekly allowance** refreshes every week
- Different plans include different allowances

Costs vary by model — more capable models use more actions than faster ones.

## Your Weekly Allowance

| Plan | Actions / week |
|------|----------------|
| Fly (Free) | 20K |
| Bee | 100K |
| Wasp | 300K |
| Hornet | Custom |

Your balance appears on the **Subscriptions** page as **remaining / total actions**, with any **purchased** credits shown on top. When you run out, it shows **Credits exhausted**.

## Buying Extra Credits

Need more before your allowance refreshes? On the Subscriptions page:

1. Open **Need More Actions?**
2. Drag the slider to choose an amount
3. The price updates live — larger bundles get a volume discount (shown as **% off**)
4. Click **Purchase Credits** to check out (secured by Stripe)

Purchased credits are added to your balance.

## Tips

- Monitor your balance on the Subscriptions page
- Use efficient models for simple tasks
- Consider a higher plan if you regularly buy extra credits
