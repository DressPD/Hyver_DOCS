## Accessing Subscriptions

Open **Subscriptions** from your account menu. The page shows your current plan, your resource usage, your credit balance, and the available plans.

## Available Plans

Hyver offers four tiers, each named after an insect. Prices are in euros (€). Usage is measured in **actions**.

### Fly — Free

- **20K actions/week**
- Basic models
- Free forever, no credit card

### Bee — €20/month

- **100K actions/week**
- Standard models
- Community support

### Wasp — €200/month ⭐ Most Popular

- **300K actions/week**
- Premium models
- Live support
- Dedicated manager

### Hornet — Custom

- **Custom action allowance**
- Premium models
- Live support and a dedicated manager
- Onboarding services and custom integrations
- **Book a demo** — pricing is tailored to your needs

## Billing Period

Toggle between **Monthly** and **Annual** at the top of the plans. Annual billing saves about **20%** (for example, Wasp is billed at €1,920/year).

## Changing Plans

- **Upgrade / Downgrade** — click the button on the plan you want; checkout opens (secured by Stripe)
- **Hornet** — click **Book a demo** and the team will follow up
- **Cancel** — from your current-plan card, click **Cancel plan**. Access continues until the end of the period, and you can **Undo cancel** any time before it ends.
