# Create Your Account

## Sign Up

1. Go to the Hyver landing page
2. Click **Get Started** or **Sign Up**
3. Fill in your details:
   - **First name** and **Surname** — 2–50 characters each (letters, spaces, hyphens, and apostrophes)
   - **Email** - A valid email address
   - **Password** - Must meet security requirements:
     - At least 8 characters
     - Include an uppercase letter
     - Include a lowercase letter
     - Include at least one number

   The form shows a live strength meter and ticks off each requirement as you type.

4. Click **Sign Up**

## Confirm Your Email

Depending on configuration, Hyver may ask you to verify your email:

- If a confirmation is required, you're taken to a **Confirm** screen
- Check your inbox for a verification code and enter it to activate your account
- Otherwise, you're signed in automatically once the account is created
